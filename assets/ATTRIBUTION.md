# Assets

- `rooftop.png`: original artwork generated for Radio Anime with OpenAI image generation, 2026-09-10. No existing anime character was used as a reference.
- `manrope-regular.ttf`, `manrope-bold.ttf`: Manrope by the Manrope Project Authors, obtained from Google Fonts. License in `Manrope-LICENSE.txt`.
- `favicon.svg` and interface icons: original simple SVG geometry for this project.
- Anime cover art and video URLs: provided by the AnimeThemes API. They remain external and belong to their respective rights holders. Credits and a link to AnimeThemes are visible in the app.
- `data/selection.json`: metadata snapshot fetched from the public AnimeThemes API on 2026-09-10. Video files are not bundled or cached. Entries marked NSFW or spoilers are excluded by the app.
